# Privacy Policy — Template

_Effective 13 November 2026_

Template (package `org.prauga.template`) is an app published by Saalim Quadri for PVOT-OSS. This
policy explains what the app does with your data.

**In short: the app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It has no internet
permission, so nothing you do in it leaves your phone.**

## What the app stores

The items you add, kept in the app's memory while it runs.

## Permissions

None.

## Backups

Android may include the app's data in your device backup and in a transfer to a new device.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published with the app's source and take effect with the release that
ships them.

## Contact

Open an issue at <https://github.com/PVOT-OSS/pvot-app-template/issues>.
